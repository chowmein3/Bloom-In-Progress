BLOOM IN PROGRESS
a garden for two

START
  Windows: double-click BloomInProgress.exe
           The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
           Click "More info", then "Run anyway". (The game is not signed,
           which costs money; that is all the warning means.)
  Mac:     right-click (or Control-click) "Bloom In Progress" and choose
           Open, then Open again. On newer macOS, if there is no Open
           button: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
           (Same reason: the game is not signed.) If macOS asks to let the
           game find devices on your network, click Allow.
  Linux:   run BloomInProgress.x86_64

PLAY TOGETHER
  One of you hosts, the other joins. You each need the game.

  Host:     Co-op > Host > pick a garden (or "Host a new garden").
  Partner:  Co-op > Join.

  If the host sees "Invite code: ABC-DEF":
            Send that code to your partner (it is already copied; just
            paste). The partner types or pastes it on the Join screen once.
            After that the garden is listed there and lights up whenever
            the host has it open: one click.
            The first connection of the day can take up to a minute.

  If the host sees "Hosting. Partner joins: ...":
            Same Wi-Fi: the garden shows up on the partner's Join screen by
            itself. Click it. If Windows asks about the firewall, click
            "Allow access".
            Different homes: if the host's help card (press H) shows a line
            starting "Elsewhere:", the partner types that address. If not,
            the host forwards UDP port 24565 in their router's settings.

KEYS
  WASD / arrows  move            E    backpack (seeds, crafting, hats)
  Shift (hold)   run             J    journal (plants, goals, fish)
  Space / click  use held tool   M    map on/off
  F / right click  use hand      N    music on/off
  Space and F work on the tile in front of you. Clicking works on the
  tile under the cursor when it is right next to you (diagonals too);
  farther away, on the tile in front.
  1-7            choose tool     H    help card
  8 9 0 -        hot bar
  Q              change seed     Esc  pause menu

  Tools: 1 hoe, 2 watering can, 3 seeds, 4 held item, 5 fishing rod,
  6 pickaxe (once bought from Flint in town), 7 axe. T shows emotes; Enter chats with
  your partner. Settings (name, volume, fullscreen) are in the Esc menu.
  Your hand (F) pets, picks, picks up, clears and opens things, so those
  never need a tool. If the held tool has nothing to do, Space uses the
  hand too.

GOOD TO KNOW
  Sleep:   face your tent's door and press F to go in, then face the
           bed and press F. The day
           ends when everyone is in bed. The garden is saved every night.
  Coins:   pick grown plants by hand (F) and sell them to Bristle,
           the hedgehog at the stall next to your home (face the stall,
           press Space).
  Town:    walk up the trail at the top of the garden. Shopkeepers stand
           behind their counters while open (and walk about town, visit
           the inn and go home to bed otherwise); face one and press F. Bristle
           (seeds, animals, hats), Juniper the baker, Flint the
           blacksmith (better tools, pickaxe), Marlow the fishmonger,
           Birch the carpenter (bigger home, coop, barn, furniture), Sage
           the herbalist, Professor Quill at the museum (donations),
           Mayor Clementine (notice board) and Rosie at the inn. Each
           pays extra for their own kind of goods and has requests.
           Make friends and each has a story (quests) to share; the
           mayor has errands and town projects. Journal (J) > Quests.
           Fish the river, toss a coin in the fountain for luck, enjoy
           the lookout, pick from the orchard. Walk down the trail at the
           bottom of town to go home.
  Making:  clear branches, rocks and weeds by hand (F) for wood,
           stone and fiber. In the backpack (E), the Crafting tab turns
           them into paths, fences, mulch, sprinklers and lanterns. Click
           one to hold it, press 4, then Space to set it down.
  Cooking: with the Cabin's stove, the Kitchen tab turns harvest into
           meals. Hold one (4) and press Space to eat it for energy.
  Axe:     press 7 and Space at a tree, stump or log in the garden. A
           tree takes three swings, falls over and leaves wood to pick up.
           It grows back after a season.
  Baking:  buy an oven from Juniper in town, set it down at home and press F
           at it. Grow wheat (mill it into flour in the Kitchen tab)
           and apple or banana trees; buy sugar, butter and cinnamon
           from Juniper or Bristle's store.
  Backpack: each player has their own coins and things. Drag a stack
           to move it, or onto the hot bar row (keys 8, 9, 0, -). Drag
           it out of the backpack to drop it; walk over it to pick it up.
  Clock:   the tower left of your home sets any date, time and weather,
           so you can see the garden in every season. Nothing grows or
           wilts for the time you skip.
  Fishing: face the pond or the river with the rod (5), press Space, wait for the "!",
           press Space, then press Space whenever the marker is on green.
  Bin:     the crate by your home sells what you put in it overnight.
  Goals:   the journal (J) lists goals that pay coins; day 14 of each
           season is a festival, with a party in the town plaza from 5pm
           (talk to Mayor Clementine by the fountain to join in).
  Animals: Birch in town builds a coop and barn; Bristle sells animals. Feed (F) and
           stroke (F again) each animal daily for eggs and milk.
  Visitors: someone stands near the stall most days. Talk (F) and give
           a gift a day to make friends; friends send presents.
  Mine:    the cave at the top right. Buy the pickaxe from Flint in town, press
           F at the cave mouth, then 6 and Space on rocks.
  Machines: craft a jar, press, cheese press or beehive, set it down,
           press F to fill it and F again to collect.
  Post:    the mailbox (left of home) and notice board (right of home)
           have letters, gifts and requests. Press F at each.
