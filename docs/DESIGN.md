# Bloom In Progress — design notes

A whimsical pixel-art co-op game for couples about raising a shared garden over a long
time. The genre and general farming mechanics are borrowed from cozy farm
sims; the name, plants, characters and art are our own.

## Pillars
1. **Nurture, not farm.** Plants are companions. Harvesting is optional.
2. **Together.** One shared garden; anyone can care for any plant.
3. **Slow and gentle.** Neglect sets you back, it never wipes you out.

## Core loop (built in Milestone 1)
- A day runs 6am to 2am. Growth happens overnight, when you sleep in the tent.
- Soil: grass -> tilled (hoe) -> planted (seed) -> watered. Soil dries every
  morning. Empty tilled soil sometimes turns back to grass. Rain waters everything.
- A plant grows one day for each day it was watered. Unwatered = paused.
- **Mood** (0-100, starts at 50): +4 watered, +8 petted, -12 left thirsty.
  Mood 80+ on a mature plant makes it **radiant**: special colour, sparkles,
  extra seeds, and an occasional growth spurt while growing.
- Each player can pet each plant once a day. Plants remember who cared for
  them (`caretakers`), ready for co-op bonuses.
- Seasons are 28 days. Out of season, a plant goes **dormant** (asleep, no
  growth, no mood loss). It does not die.
- Energy (100/day) limits how much one player can do. Staying up to 2am means
  waking with half energy.
- Seeds, produce and coins are shared by everyone in the garden.

## The Heart Tree (built in Milestone 3)
One shared plant beside the tent; the long-term goal of the garden.
- Each night it gains **love**: 1 per plant cared for that day (watered or
  petted, up to 12), +3 if the tree was watered, +2 per player who hugged it.
- Stages at 40, 120, 260 and 450 love: seed, sprout, sapling, young tree, in
  bloom. A typical garden reaches them around days 4, 11, 24 and 40.
- Reaching a stage drops seeds for plants of the coming season, so the tree is
  how new species enter the garden.

## Garden journal (built in Milestone 3)
Shared by both players, opened with J. Each species has three milestones:
planted, bloomed, radiant. Undiscovered plants show as a silhouette. First
blooms and radiant blooms are announced on the morning screen.

## Look and sound (built in Milestone 3)
- Sprites for plants, players, ground, tent and tree are generated by
  `tools/make_art.py`; music and effects by `tools/make_audio.py`.
- Plants show how they feel: a droplet when thirsty, a splash when watered,
  hearts when petted (a heart and a music note for a duet), z's when dormant.
- Seasons change the ground and the air: petals, fireflies on summer
  evenings, falling leaves, snow.

## Home, shop and backpack
- **Home:** walking into the home's door leads to a room inside (its own
  space, far to the side of the garden grid). The bed is the only way to end
  the day; sleepers are drawn tucked in, host on the left pillow, partner on
  the right. Anyone still up at 2am is carried to bed with half energy.
- **Coins:** shared purse, 100 to start. Each plant has a sell price and a
  seed price (`data/plants/*.tres`).
- **Bristle's stall** sells:
  - seeds for plants the garden knows (starting three, plus Heart Tree gifts);
  - hoe and watering can upgrades that reach 3 then 5 tiles in the direction
    faced, for the energy of one use;
  - the home: tent -> cabin (1500) -> cottage (5000); each level is a bigger
    room and +15 energy every morning;
  - hats, bought once for the garden and worn by either player.
- **Backpack (E):** grid of seeds and harvest, the purse, the player's
  portrait and the hat wardrobe.
- Prices and upgrade tables live in `GameState` (`UPGRADES`, `HATS`).

## Gathering, crafting, cooking, skill
Borrowed from farm sims and cut down to what serves a shared garden.
- **Debris:** a new garden starts with 18 branches, rocks and weeds, and one
  to three more appear each night (30 at most, never in the yard around the
  home). The basket clears one for 1-2 wood, stone or fiber.
- **Crafting** from the backpack: stepping stone (1 stone), fence (2 wood),
  rich mulch x2 (3 fiber), sprinkler (3 stone, 2 wood, level 2), garden
  lantern (1 of each, level 3). Placed things stand on empty garden tiles
  (not soil); the basket lifts them again. Fences, sprinklers and lanterns
  block walking; stones and debris don't.
- **Mulch** goes on tilled soil: 35% chance of an extra day's growth each
  night, and the bed never reverts to grass.
- **Sprinklers** water the four neighbouring soil tiles each morning.
- **Cooking** needs the Cabin's stove: tea (2 moonbell, +30 energy), salad
  (sunpuff + dewcup, +45), stew (2 gigglecap + pricklepal, +75), pie
  (lanternsquash + snowpip, +110). Meals are eaten from the item slot.
- **Gardening level** (shared, 1-10): +1 experience per plant cared for each
  night, +8 per harvest (+4 if radiant), +1 per debris, +2 per craft, +20 per
  request. Levels unlock recipes and add 2 energy each.
- **Requests:** each morning without one, Bristle asks for 2-4 of an
  in-season plant the garden knows and pays double.
- Items, placed things, experience and the request are shared and saved; the
  tool bar's sixth slot holds one placeable thing or meal.

Not built (yet): fishing, mining, animals, villagers and festivals.

## Clock tower
A tower left of the home whose face shows the garden's real time. Using it
opens a screen to set the season, day, year, time (10-minute steps, 6am to
1:50am) and weather. Pressing CHANGE TIME! jumps the calendar and clock
without simulating the days between: nothing grows, wilts or is saved early;
plants only wake or go dormant for the new season, and rain waters the soil.
The jump plays a four-second, deliberately overblown animation (racing sky,
sun and moon chasing round a spinning clock, flying calendar pages, a flash,
then a calm "It is now..."), on both players' screens in co-op.

Players run while Shift is held (1.75x). The walk is a four-step cycle (left
foot, together, right foot, together) with arms swinging opposite.

## Menus
The title menu follows the usual farm-sim flow: **New** (name a garden),
**Load** (list of saved gardens with their date, with delete), **Co-op**
(**Host** a saved or new garden, or **Join** by address) and **Exit**. Esc in
game opens a pause menu with Resume, Return to title and Exit. Each garden is
its own save file, written every night; alone, pausing stops the clock, in
co-op it does not.

## HUD
Kept small so the garden stays in view: a toggleable minimap top left (M), an
info box top right (date, time and weather, Heart Tree love), a vertical
energy bar bottom right, and an icon tool bar bottom centre. Coins and
Heart Tree love sit under the clock.

## Co-op (built in Milestone 2)
- Two players. One hosts and owns the garden and its save; the other joins by
  address. Players have stable slots (host 1, guest 2) so plants remember them.
- The host is in charge: a guest's actions are requests the host checks and
  applies, then every change is mirrored back. The host also runs the clock.
- A late joiner receives the whole garden.
- The day ends when both players are in the tent. At the 2am curfew, anyone
  not in bed wakes with half energy.
- **Duet:** if two different players pet the same plant on one day it gets +6
  mood on top of the normal +8.
- Seeds, produce and the garden are shared; energy is per player.

## Getting it and connecting
- Players download one zip and double-click the game; nothing to install.
- **Relay (when set up):** both games connect out to a small relay server, so
  no router setup or addresses. Each garden has a permanent six-letter invite
  code. The partner enters it once; from then on their Join screen lists the
  garden and shows when it is open.
- **Without the relay:** on the same network the host announces itself and
  appears on the Join screen; across the internet the host asks the router to
  open the port (UPnP) and shows the address to share.
